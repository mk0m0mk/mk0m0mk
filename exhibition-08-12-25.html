artist statement

"Disco 2000" a sequence of thirty-five matte black and white inkjet prints with varying exposure.

Inspired by Zoe Crosher's work, "The Vanishing of Michelle Dubois". Title from the Pulp song by the same name.

7x5 grid, printed on Epson Enhanced Matte paper, 8x10" prints matted onto 11x14" frames. Noel Waldron pictured alongside.
