artist statement

"data center protoype" is a proof of concept that tests machine learning based pose and distance estimation. The piece constantly emits the noise pollution heard by nearby residents of the Vantage V2 Data Center in Sterling, Virginia.

As a subject comes into the area of estimation, their distance from the speaker is constantly calculated and directly influences the volume of the noise. Max distance is 3.55 meters (quietest), minimum distance is 1 meter (loudest).
