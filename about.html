VICTOR M. GONZALEZ

Victor M. Gonzalez is an artist and photographer based in Southern California. He is currently studying Photography and Media at California Institute of the Arts.

Working across analog and digital photography, installation, sound, and material-based processes, Gonzalez approaches image-making as both documentation and physical practice. His work moves between observations of public space, portraiture, photographic experimentation, and constructed forms of presentation.

His practice is particularly interested in the relationship between photographs and the environments in which they are made, displayed, reproduced, and encountered. Alongside image-making, he works with alternative photographic processes and installation strategies that extend photographs beyond the conventional print.
