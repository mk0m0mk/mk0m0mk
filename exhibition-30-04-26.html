artist statement

"Recreación de la Trayectoria de Vuelo de un OVNI - 12/06/2011" is a retelling of the artist's family immigration from Tequila, Jalisco to (its coincedentally sister city) Cathedral City, California.

The photos are sequenced left to right by geographical location starting with Mexicali and ending in their then home. This work is heavily influenced by the New Topographics movement, framing each location in a sterile, deadpan manner juxtaposing the otherwise intimate journey.

Shot on 4x5 Portra 400 using a Cambo SC. Developed by House of 35 and scanned via Epson V850 Pro. Printed on Epson Enhanced Matte and Epson Hot Press Bright. Held up with screws and extra large binder clips.
